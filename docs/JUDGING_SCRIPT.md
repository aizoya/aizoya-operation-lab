## Spoken pitch

“I'm Franklyn Rosario, MBA-ITM, Founder and CEO of AIZOYA.

A catering quote can look profitable and still promise food the kitchen cannot deliver. AIZOYA puts five specialist checks behind one decision: can we fulfill this order at the required contribution margin?

Here is a representative fifty-guest chicken order. Operations checks preparation time. Sales checks demand and budget. Kitchen checks stock and capacity. Finance calculates costs and margin. Quality reconciles the results.

The requested order is blocked: there is not enough stock. We don't pretend the food exists. The system proposes a smaller draft for eleven guests, with twelve servings including the buffer. This changes the customer's request and needs their agreement.

We rerun every check. The proposed price is $239.26 before tax, with $167.48 estimated cost and $71.78 contribution—a thirty percent contribution margin, not net profit.

The Masky workers explain these findings in clearly labeled prerecorded clips. The checks run live using deterministic code; live AI inference is not connected.

The operator can inspect the evidence and record a local review of this exact proposal. Recipe, handling, allergens and tax still need human verification.

Our commercial hypothesis is faster catering proposals with fewer costly promises. We haven't measured customer savings yet. Today, the working result is a feasible, costed alternative with an inspectable record.”

## Likely judge questions

- **Are these live AI agents?** Five constrained deterministic workers execute the dashboard checks. Five eve AI definitions compile separately but are not connected to this flow. Masky provides prerecorded presentations of the exact recovery.
- **Does eleven guests solve the fifty-person order?** It is a feasible alternative, requiring customer agreement. It does not fulfill the original order; replenishment or a different menu needs separately verified data.
- **Is $71.78 profit?** It is estimated contribution under representative cost assumptions. It is not verified net profit or realized revenue.
- **How do you prevent a bad quote?** Typed contracts, integer-cent calculations, recomputation, stock/capacity/freshness blockers, bounded retries and a review tied to the exact proposal hash.
- **What happens if a tool fails?** A bounded transient failure retries. A hard timeout or missing essential data blocks the mission with saved evidence.
- **Is this production ready or security cleared?** No. The local prototype has unresolved Snyk and dependency findings and no production authentication or cross-booking inventory control.
- **What is the business model?** Test restaurant willingness to pay for faster, better checked catering proposals. Customer ROI and demand remain hypotheses.
