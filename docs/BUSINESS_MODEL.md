# Commercial model — structural case, not validated revenue

## Buyer and objective

Proposed buyer: restaurant/catering owner or operations manager responsible for
quotes, kitchen capacity and order contribution. Initial potential pilot: LBK,
subject to a separate redacted-data verification step. No real LBK inventory,
costs, recipes, bookings or customer data were imported. This is an independent
product, not a change to PlateReach, Toast, LBK's website or production systems.

Known: Franklyn requested a sellable operations product and a catering journey.
Inferred: faster feasible proposals and fewer margin mistakes could justify
payment. Missing: confirmed buying process, willingness to pay, manual baseline,
proposal frequency, historical corrections, lost orders and operating costs.

## Product offer hypothesis

One service request becomes a documented feasibility and pre-tax contribution
proposal with human review. Start with restaurant catering; expand only when
validated templates and customer demand support another service category.
Five agents are implementation detail. The customer buys a faster, more reliable
decision and operational handoff, not an agent count.

Proposed price test: $199/location/month with an explicitly negotiated onboarding
scope; usage and provider cost caps must be set before offering terms. This is a
research hypothesis, not published pricing, a contract, a charge or founder approval.
Do not reuse PlateReach's separate approved pricing as this product's authority.

## Measurement and finance

Productivity value/month = proposals/month × minutes saved/proposal ÷ 60 ×
loaded hourly labor cost × adoption. Additional contribution = attributable
incremental accepted orders × actual contribution/order. Avoid counting the same
labor savings twice inside contribution. Avoided errors = verified prevented
errors × actual average loss, tracked separately from uncertain risk projections.

Net monthly benefit = verified incremental value − subscription − provider usage
− support/onboarding allocation − infrastructure. All required input values are
currently missing. Low/base/high ROI therefore remains structural, not numerical.
No inference that one synthetic quote represents realized profit or ROI.

The sample 50-guest scenario yields $585.75 modeled costs, $836.79 proposed price
and $251.04 contribution before tax on purely representative inputs. This is
calculation evidence only. It excludes a validated tax model and all real operating
verification; it is not LBK pricing or an actual customer offer.

Actual recorded business outcomes: 0 real pilot customers, $0 realized revenue;
time savings and conversion rate unknown. Runtime timing is measured in
`evidence/acceptance-report.json`; infrastructure cost is unmeasured, and no live
provider calls were made. Missing provider prices do not count as zero cost.

## Commercial readiness gates

Before selling: connect and evaluate live AI if marketed as AI; resolve security
findings; perform independent review; establish production identity/tenant
isolation, secure deployment and recovery; import and reconcile approved pilot
inputs; validate stock/capacity across bookings; settle privacy, retention,
support, tax and commercial terms; test actual iPhone/Safari use; measure customer
baseline and correction effort. This candidate is not yet ready for paid production.

First validation plan: time a real redacted inquiry manually, then with the
product; have the operator review every cost and feasibility result. Record time,
corrections, missed issues, source freshness and operator acceptance. Propose a
paid pilot only after those gates clear. No outreach or commitment is authorized
by this document.
